# IELTS Academic Test Format

Reference checked on 8 October 2026. Total test time is approximately 2 hours and 45 minutes. Listening, Reading, and Writing are completed in one session; check the booking confirmation for the separate Speaking appointment.

| Section | Time | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | Approximately 30 minutes | Four parts, 40 questions; recordings played once |
| Academic Reading | 60 minutes | Three reading passages, 40 questions |
| Academic Writing | 60 minutes | Task 1 and Task 2 |
| Speaking | 11–14 minutes | Three-part interview with an examiner |

## Listening

1. A conversation in an everyday social context.
2. A monologue in an everyday social context.
3. A conversation in an educational or training context.
4. A monologue on an academic subject.

Question types include multiple choice, matching, completion, and map or diagram labelling. Follow each answer's word limit and check spelling. Practise with computer-format timing rather than assuming paper-test answer-transfer time applies.

## Academic Reading

Common question types include True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given, matching headings or information, completion, and multiple choice. Enter all answers within the 60-minute limit; there is no additional answer-transfer time.

## Academic Writing

- **Task 1:** Describe visual information such as a chart, table, map, or process. Write at least **150 words**; allocate approximately **20 minutes**.
- **Task 2:** Write an essay responding to a viewpoint, argument, or problem. Write at least **250 words**; allocate approximately **40 minutes**.
- Task 2 carries **twice the weight** of Task 1 in the Writing score.

## Speaking

- **Part 1 (4–5 minutes):** Introduction and questions about familiar topics, including work, study, and interests.
- **Part 2 (3–4 minutes):** A topic card, one minute of preparation, a one-to-two-minute talk, and brief follow-up questions.
- **Part 3 (4–5 minutes):** A broader discussion related to the Part 2 topic.

## Preparation for the Target Computer Test

- Practise reading passages on screen and entering answers accurately.
- Type both Writing tasks under timed conditions.
- Speaking is an interview with a human examiner; confirm the appointment time and delivery arrangements with the provider.
